🔗 No-Screw Connections

1
Base ↔ Column — Dovetail Press-Fit
Tapered dovetail pin on column bottom slides into base socket. Friction holds it. Pull straight up to release.
⎯
Column Bottom ↔ Top — Interlocking Sleeve
Column halves join with a 40mm internal sleeve that press-fits into both ends. No glue needed with PETG.
2
Column ↔ Arm — Snap-Tab T-Slot
Arm slides into T-slot on column top. Spring tab clicks into detent. Squeeze to release.
3
Camera ↔ Pocket — Snug Slide-In Sleeve
Fold the C920 clip completely closed and slide it vertically into the snug sleeve. The camera neck slides through the front slot. Holds gravity-locked & aligned.
